Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 • How MiMusic.net protects your audio files, browser permissions, and personal data

1. Introduction

Welcome to MiMusic.net (https://mimusic.net/). Protecting your privacy while you use our browser-based audio utilities and read our mobile playback guides is a core priority. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit our site, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

100% Client-Side Audio & Video Processing: Every audio utility on MiMusic.net—including our Volume Booster, Audio Trimmer, Pitch Shifter, and Video-to-Audio Converter—runs locally inside your web browser using the Web Audio API. Your media files are never uploaded to, stored on, or viewed by our servers.

2. Microphone & Hardware Access

Certain diagnostic tools on our platform, such as the Microphone Tester and Decibel Meter, require temporary access to your device's microphone to visualize live sound levels. Your web browser explicitly asks for your permission before enabling the microphone. All audio input is analyzed strictly in real-time local memory and is never recorded, saved, or transmitted over the Internet.

3. Log Files & Website Analytics

Like most modern websites, MiMusic.net uses standard server log files and privacy-conscious analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors interact with our pages and to improve tool performance. The technical data collected may include:

  • Browser type, device category, and operating system
  • Referring and exit pages, date/time stamps, and aggregate page views
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP) and anonymized IP address metrics

This information is used solely for analyzing trends, diagnosing technical issues, and optimizing site speed. It is never linked to personally identifiable information.

4. Cookies & Local Preferences

MiMusic.net uses cookies and standard browser storage to remember basic interface preferences and ensure smooth performance across visits. You can disable, block, or clear cookies at any time through your individual browser settings without losing access to our free audio tools.

5. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

To keep all audio tools and guides free for everyone, MiMusic.net partners with third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense, to display advertisements:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the DoubleClick advertising cookie) to serve relevant ads based on a user's prior visits to MiMusic.net or other websites across the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or by managing your preferences at www.aboutads.info.

6. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Rights

Depending on your location—including residents of the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom under GDPR, and California under CCPA—you have specific data protection rights:

  • The right to request details about any personal data we may hold about you.
  • The right to request correction or deletion of personal information you have voluntarily shared with us (such as via email).
  • The right to opt out of personalized advertising cookies.

To exercise any of these rights, please reach out to us directly at info@mimusic.net.

7. Children's Privacy

MiMusic.net does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately at info@mimusic.net so we can promptly remove it from our records.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new audio utilities, legal requirements, or advertising disclosures. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
