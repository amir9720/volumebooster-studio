Pitch & Tempo Shifter

Modify the speed (tempo) and key (pitch) of any music file independently. Ideal for musicians, transcribers, and singers.

🎚️

How to Use the Shifter

1

Select Your Track

Upload the song or backing track you want to modify. MP3 and WAV formats provide the best processing results.

2

Adjust Tempo

Use the slider to slow down the song (for practicing an instrument or learning a language) or speed it up.

3

Pitch Control

By default, we preserve the original pitch. If you select "No", the pitch will drop as the song slows down, creating a deep, stretched effect.

4

Live Playback

Hit play to hear your modifications instantly. Changes are applied in real-time within your web browser.
