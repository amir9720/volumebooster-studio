Audio Latency & Sync Test

Test your wireless Bluetooth earbuds for lag. Check if the visual flashes perfectly match the audio beeps on your device.

Connect your Bluetooth device and click start.

How to Test Your Earbuds

1

Connect Earbuds

Ensure your wireless headphones or TWS earbuds are paired and connected to your smartphone or PC.

2

Start the Metronome

Click "Start Sync Test". The central dot will begin flashing bright green exactly once every second.

3

Look and Listen

Focus on the flashing dot. The audio system will trigger a sharp "tick" sound precisely at the same millisecond the color changes.

4

Analyze the Delay

If you hear the tick slightly *after* you see the green flash, your Bluetooth headphones suffer from audio latency.

Frequently Asked Questions

Wireless audio data has to be compressed by your phone, transmitted through the air, received by your earbuds, decompressed, and played. This conversion process takes time (usually between 100ms and 300ms), causing a noticeable delay when watching videos or gaming.
If your earbuds have a dedicated "Gaming Mode" or "Low Latency Mode", turn it on. Alternatively, ensure both your phone and earbuds support modern, fast codecs like aptX Low Latency (aptX LL) or Bluetooth 5.2. Unfortunately, cheap earbuds with basic SBC codecs will always have lag.
When listening to pure audio (like Spotify or Apple Music), there is no visual component for your brain to compare the sound to. Latency only becomes obvious when there is a visual cue, such as an actor's lips moving or a gunshot in a video game.
