Why Check Your Hearing Frequency Range?

Human hearing typically spans from 20 Hz (very low bass) up to 20,000 Hz (very high pitch). However, as we age or experience exposure to loud environments and headphones, our ability to hear high frequencies diminishes. Checking your hearing range regularly helps you monitor ear health.

The MiMusic Hearing Test uses precise Web Audio oscillators to generate pure test tones across custom selectable frequencies. By wearing headphones in a quiet room, you can test each frequency band individually to discover your upper hearing limit.

How to Take the Hearing Test (Step-by-Step)

Follow these simple instructions to test your ears safely and accurately.

Put on headphones, select a frequency button, and click play.

  • Step 1: Wear Headphones. Always use quality headphones or earbuds for accurate frequency isolation.
  • Step 2: Select a Frequency. Choose from the frequency selector buttons (ranging from deep 250Hz up to ultra-high 16,000Hz).
  • Step 3: Play and Listen. Click "Play Tone" to verify if you can clearly hear the sound in that frequency range.
