How to Test Your Hearing Range

1

Lower Your Volume

Before pressing play, turn your smartphone or computer volume down to 10-20% to prevent sudden loud noises.

2

Start at Mid-Range

Begin the test around 4,000 Hz or 8,000 Hz. Press "Start Tone" and verify that you can comfortably hear the beep.

3

Increase Frequency

Slowly drag the slider to the right. As the number increases, the pitch will get higher and higher.

4

Find Your Limit

Stop sliding when the sound disappears for you. That number indicates the maximum high-frequency limit of your current hearing.
