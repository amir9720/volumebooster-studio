What is 8D Audio?

If you've spent any time on YouTube or TikTok recently, you've likely come across tracks labeled as "8D Audio." But what exactly does that mean? Standard music is mixed in "stereo," meaning the audio is split into a left channel and a right channel. When you listen with headphones, the band sounds like they are playing directly inside your head.

8D Audio (which technically refers to 3-dimensional spatial panning) takes that stereo track and manipulates it using psychoacoustic effects. By automating the panning from left to right and adding specific reverb, the music creates an illusion of physical space. It tricks your brain into thinking you are standing in the middle of a massive concert hall, with the artist literally walking in circles around you while performing.
