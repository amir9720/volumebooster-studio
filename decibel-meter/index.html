Understanding Decibels and Sound Pressure

Sound is measured in decibels (dB), a logarithmic unit used to express the ratio of a given sound pressure against a standard baseline. Normal human breathing registers around 10 dB, a quiet conversation sits around 60 dB, while a rock concert or siren can easily exceed 100 dB to 120 dB.

The MiMusic Decibel Meter transforms your smartphone or computer microphone into a reliable sound level indicator. Whether you want to check how loud your room is, test your headphones, or ensure your music listening habits are safe for your ears, our browser-based tool gives you instant measurements.

How to Use the Decibel Meter (Step-by-Step)

Using our sound level meter requires no app installation; it runs safely within your browser.

Grant microphone permission and view real-time decibel levels.

  • Step 1: Start the Meter. Click the "Start Sound Meter" button.
  • Step 2: Grant Permission. Allow your browser access to your device microphone when prompted.
  • Step 3: Monitor Noise Levels. Watch the digital meter update in real-time as sound enters your microphone.

Protecting Your Hearing

Prolonged exposure to sounds above 85 dB can cause permanent hearing damage over time. Use this meter to keep your environment and music listening levels safe.
