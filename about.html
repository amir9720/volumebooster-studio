About Mi Music App

Discover what makes this default audio player the preferred choice for millions of Android users. A deep dive into its core features and capabilities.

Developed by Xiaomi Inc., the Mi Music application serves as the primary gateway for local audio playback across the entire MIUI and HyperOS ecosystem. However, it is not just restricted to Xiaomi phones; its global version is available for various Android devices, offering a lightweight, battery-efficient alternative to streaming-heavy applications.

Key App Features

🎛️

Advanced Equalizer

Built-in integration with the system's "Mi Sound Enhancer", providing granular EQ bands and presets tailored for specific earphone models.

✂️

Ringtone Cutter

A native editing utility that allows you to trim any song and instantly set it as your device's ringtone without downloading third-party editors.

📜

Dynamic Lyrics Sync

Automatically scans for and displays .lrc files located in your music folders, creating a karaoke-like experience directly on your lock screen.

🌙

Sleep Timer

Set a countdown timer before going to bed. The app will automatically pause playback and prevent battery drain while you sleep.
