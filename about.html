About Mi Music

Welcome to MiMusic.net, your premier online destination for client-side digital signal processing (DSP) utilities and comprehensive mobile audio guides. Our mission is to make advanced audio processing accessible to everyone directly through the browser, without mandatory app downloads or subscriptions.

Our Core Mission

Traditional audio modification often requires heavy software installations, costly licenses, or cloud converters that upload your personal media to third-party servers. We created Mi Music to revolutionize that workflow: harnessing the powerful Web Audio API to process all audio calculations directly inside your device's memory (RAM), ensuring zero bandwidth waste and uncompromising privacy.

Privacy-First Audio DSP

Whether you are cleaning water from phone speaker grilles, converting videos to MP3, transforming vocal pitch, or measuring room noise, your data remains 100% on your device. We do not store, listen to, or log your personal media files.
