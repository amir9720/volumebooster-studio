About the Mi Music App

The Mi Music app is the official, pre-installed media player on all Xiaomi, Redmi, and POCO smartphones running MIUI and HyperOS. It is designed to offer a seamless, ad-light (depending on region) local listening experience.

Core Capabilities

  • Extensive Format Support: Natively decodes MP3, FLAC, WAV, AAC, and OGG files without requiring third-party codecs.
  • Built-in Equalizer (Mi Sound): Integrates directly with MIUI's hardware sound enhancer to provide presets tailored specifically for Xiaomi earbuds and headphones.
  • Lyrics Sync: Automatically displays and syncs local LRC files so you can sing along to your tracks.
  • Sleep Timer: Set a countdown to automatically stop playback when you fall asleep.

Why It Stands Out

Unlike heavy streaming apps, Mi Music focuses on your local offline library. It consumes minimal RAM and battery life, making it the perfect companion for long listening sessions on the go. If you need advanced audio editing (like boosting volume or reversing tracks), you can supplement the app using our free online web tools.
