About Mi Music Features

Discover the core capabilities and offline playback advantages of the default audio player for Xiaomi devices.

The Ultimate Local Media Player

The Mi Music app is the official, pre-installed media player on all Xiaomi, Redmi, and POCO smartphones running MIUI and HyperOS. It is designed to offer a seamless, ad-light (depending on region) local listening experience focusing on high-quality offline playback.

Core Capabilities

  • Extensive Format Support: Natively decodes MP3, FLAC, WAV, AAC, and OGG files without requiring third-party audio codecs.
  • Built-in Equalizer (Mi Sound): Integrates directly with MIUI's hardware sound enhancer to provide presets tailored specifically for Xiaomi earbuds and wired headphones.
  • Lyrics Sync: Automatically displays and syncs local LRC files so you can follow along with your tracks.
  • Sleep Timer & Ringtone Cutter: Set a countdown to automatically stop playback when you fall asleep, or easily trim tracks to make custom ringtones.

Why Focus on Offline Playback?

Unlike heavy subscription-based streaming apps that constantly drain battery and mobile data, Mi Music focuses heavily on your local offline library. It consumes minimal RAM, making it the perfect companion for long listening sessions on the go. If you need advanced audio editing (like boosting volume or reversing tracks), you can supplement the app using our free online web tools.
