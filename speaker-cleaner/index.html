Speaker Cleaner

Fix muffled sound by ejecting trapped water and dust. This tool plays a specific low-frequency tone designed to create maximum speaker vibration.

🔊
⚠️ Important Instruction: Turn your device volume to 100% MAXIMUM and place your phone screen-down on a flat surface before starting.

About This Tool: How Does Sound Clean Speakers?

If you've ever dropped your phone in the sink, taken it into the shower, or used it in a dusty workshop, you've probably noticed that your audio suddenly sounds muffled, distorted, or completely silent. This happens because water droplets and debris get physically trapped inside the microscopic mesh of your smartphone's speaker grill. Because water is denser than air, it blocks the sound waves from escaping.

The MiMusic Speaker Cleaner operates on a scientifically proven acoustic principle. It uses the Web Audio API to generate a specific, continuous sweep of low-frequency sine waves (usually oscillating between 165Hz and 250Hz). These specific frequencies are known to create the most intense physical vibrations in small mobile phone drivers. The acoustic pressure physically pushes the water droplets and dry dust out of the speaker cavity, restoring crystal-clear audio playback. In fact, this is the exact same sonic technology used by modern water-resistant smartwatches to clear their internals after a swim.

How to Use the Water Ejector (Step-by-Step)

To get the best results and ensure all water is fully ejected, follow these exact steps. It requires no downloads and runs directly in your browser.

Ensure your volume is at 100% before clicking the start button.

  • Step 1: Maximize Device Volume. Turn your Android or iPhone media volume all the way up to 100%. The tool relies entirely on the physical vibration of the speaker driver; lower volumes won't produce enough force to push the water out.
  • Step 2: Position Your Phone. Gravity is your friend here. Place your device screen-down on a flat, dry surface, or hold it vertically so the bottom speakers (where the charging port is) are pointing downwards toward the floor.
  • Step 3: Start the Cleaning Cycle. Tap the blue "Start Cleaning Process" button above. You will immediately hear a loud, buzzing sound. Let it play continuously for 30 to 60 seconds. You may literally see tiny water droplets escaping the grill.
  • Step 4: Wipe and Test. After a minute, stop the sound. Gently wipe any visible moisture off the bottom of the phone with a dry microfiber cloth. Play a song in the Mi Music app to test the clarity. If it still sounds slightly muffled, repeat the process 2 more times.

Is the Sweeping Tone Safe for My Phone?

Yes, completely safe. The frequencies generated by this tool (around 165Hz) fall well within the normal operating range of standard smartphone speakers. It is essentially just a very loud, specific bass note. It will not blow out your speakers or damage the internal hardware, provided you aren't leaving it running for hours on end. However, please remember that if your phone was submerged in salt water or sticky liquids (like soda), sound waves cannot remove the sticky residue, and you may need a professional physical cleaning.
