Speaker Cleaner Assistant

Try our 165Hz acoustic wave generator, designed to dislodge trapped water droplets and fine dust from your phone's speaker grills.

💧
Important Disclaimer: This tool generates audio frequencies to vibrate speaker components. It is provided "as is" and does not guarantee the successful removal of water or dust. If your device has suffered significant liquid exposure, turn it off immediately.

How to Use the Speaker Cleaner

1

Maximize Volume

Ensure your smartphone volume is set to 100%. Turn off silent or do-not-disturb modes.

2

Face Speakers Down

Hold your phone with the speaker grills facing downward to allow gravity to pull moisture out.

3

Run Audio Pulse

Click "Start Audio Pulse" and let the 165Hz tone play for about 60 to 90 seconds.

4

Wipe & Repeat

Gently wipe the grill with a dry cloth. If the sound is still muffled, repeat the process.
