How to Enhance Vocals

1

Upload the Track

Choose an audio file that sounds muddy or where the speaker is hard to hear over background noise.

2

Smart Equalization

Our tool instantly applies a parametric EQ. We boost the mid-range (1kHz - 4kHz) while slightly dipping the muddy bass frequencies.

3

Listen Instantly

Press play to hear the difference. The vocals should now cut through the mix, making speech much more intelligible.

4

100% Private

Your recorded lectures or personal voice notes remain secure because the filtering happens locally in your device's RAM.
