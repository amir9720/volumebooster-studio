Why Convert Video to Audio?

Have you ever recorded a fantastic live performance on your smartphone, downloaded a music video clip, or received a long video recording containing an important speech or interview, and wished you could just listen to it like a regular podcast or MP3? While video files are great for visual memories, they consume massive amounts of storage space and drain your phone's battery when played in the background.

The MiMusic Video to Audio Converter solves this by extracting the pure audio track from your video files. Because the native Mi Music app is optimized for lightweight local audio playback, converting your favorite music videos into standalone audio files lets you build a personalized offline playlist without wasting gigabytes of device storage.

How to Use the Video Converter (Step-by-Step)

Our tool makes extraction seamless. Because everything runs locally within your browser's HTML5 media engine, your private video files are never uploaded to any external server.

Select your video file and hit play to listen to the extracted audio track.

  • Step 1: Choose Your Video. Tap the upload box and select any standard video format from your device storage, including MP4, MOV, or WebM.
  • Step 2: Initialize Extraction. Once the video is loaded, the player extracts the internal audio stream automatically.
  • Step 3: Play and Enjoy. Click the "Play Extracted Audio" button to listen to your track instantly through your headphones or speakers.

Privacy and Security Guaranteed

Many online converters force you to upload large video files to sketchy cloud servers, risking your personal data and consuming your internet bandwidth. Our client-side application decodes the media locally inside your browser's RAM, ensuring absolute privacy and lightning-fast performance.
