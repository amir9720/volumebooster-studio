Installing & Updating Mi Music

Mi Music comes pre-installed on Xiaomi devices, but maintaining the latest version is crucial for security patches, new equalizer presets, and improved file scanning speed.

Method 1: Using the System Updater (Recommended)

The safest way to update the app is through MIUI's native system apps updater:

  1. Open your phone's Settings app.
  2. Tap on System apps updater.
  3. Scroll through the list to find Music.
  4. Tap the Update button. The installation will happen automatically.

Method 2: Via GetApps Store

If the system updater isn't showing the latest version, you can force a check using the official Xiaomi app store:

  1. Open the GetApps application on your home screen.
  2. Tap on the Me tab at the bottom right.
  3. Select App updates.
  4. Find Mi Music and tap update.

Is it available for non-Xiaomi phones?

While the app is optimized for MIUI, a limited version of Mi Music is occasionally made available on the Google Play Store for other Android devices. However, you will not have access to the hardware-level "Mi Sound Enhancer" on non-Xiaomi devices.
