Installation & Update Guide

Everything you need to know about installing the global version of Mi Music on any Android smartphone, keeping it updated, and avoiding malicious software.

Good to know: If you own a Xiaomi, Redmi, or POCO device running MIUI or HyperOS, the app is already built into your system. You only need to follow the update instructions.

Method 1: The Official Google Play Store (Recommended)

The safest and most reliable way to get the app on a non-Xiaomi Android device is directly through the Google Play Store. This ensures you receive the stable global build that has been verified by Google Play Protect.

1

Open the Play Store

Launch the Google Play Store app on your Android smartphone or tablet.

2

Search for the App

Tap the search bar at the top and type "Mi Music". Look for the official app published by "Xiaomi Inc."

3

Install & Grant Permissions

Tap "Install". Upon opening the app for the first time, you must grant it "Storage" or "Media" permissions so it can scan your local files and build your audio library.

📱 Download from Google Play

Method 2: Updating via System Apps Updater

If you are using a Xiaomi ecosystem device, the app rarely updates through the Google Play Store. Instead, Xiaomi uses its own internal update framework to push new features.

1

Go to Settings

Open your phone's main "Settings" menu.

2

Find System Apps Updater

Tap on "System apps updater" (usually located near the top of the settings list).

3

Check and Update

If an update for the music player is available, it will be listed here. Tap "Update" next to it.

⚠️ Security Warning Regarding APK Sites: We strongly advise against downloading the application via third-party APK aggregator websites. Modified or "cracked" versions found on unofficial sites often contain malware, aggressive adware, or spyware that compromises your device's security.
