Reverse Audio Engine

Flip your music backwards instantly. Find subliminal messages or create cool rewinding sound effects right in your browser.

⏪

How to Reverse Your Audio

1

Upload the File

Select any music file or voice recording from your device. Smaller files (under 10MB) process much faster.

2

Browser Decoding

We read the digital data of your file and convert it into thousands of raw audio arrays directly inside your browser memory.

3

Array Inversion

Our algorithm instantly flips the arrays mathematically, putting the very last second of the audio at the absolute beginning.

4

Instant Playback

Click play. The audio will play completely backwards without you ever having to upload it to an external server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Because we value your privacy, we don't upload your file to a powerful cloud server. Instead, we use your device's CPU and RAM to decode the MP3 file, flip every single data point, and load it back. On older phones, this might take 3 to 5 seconds.
Backward masking is a recording technique where a sound or message is recorded backward onto a track that is meant to be played forward. It was heavily debated in the 1970s and 80s rock music scene. This tool allows you to easily investigate these myths yourself.
Since the file is loaded into your browser's RAM, trying to reverse a 1-hour long podcast (50MB+) might crash your browser tab due to memory limits. We recommend using files under 10 minutes in length.
