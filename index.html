What is the Mi Music App?

Developed by Xiaomi Inc., Mi Music is the native, pre-installed audio and media playback application found across the MIUI and HyperOS ecosystem. Designed to be lightweight and battery-efficient, it serves millions of users globally as their primary offline music manager. Unlike streaming giants like Spotify or Apple Music, this application focuses heavily on local media playback, allowing users to organize downloaded MP3s, FLAC files, and audiobooks without requiring a constant internet connection.

While originally exclusive to Xiaomi, Redmi, and POCO devices, the global version of the application can now be side-loaded or downloaded via the Google Play Store on almost any modern Android device, bringing its clean interface and built-in equalizer to a broader audience.

Core Features & Capabilities

The application is packed with native tools designed to enhance the listening experience. Here are the standout features that make it a favorite among Android enthusiasts:

🎛️

Advanced EQ & Dirac

Deep integration with Xiaomi's "Mi Sound Enhancer" and Dirac HD sound profiles, allowing you to tailor the audio output specifically for different types of headphones and earbuds.

✂️

Native Ringtone Cutter

Don't download extra bloatware. The app includes a built-in audio trimming tool, letting you isolate the chorus of any song and instantly set it as your ringtone or alarm.

🌙

Sleep Timer & Dark Mode

Perfect for nighttime listening. Set a countdown timer to automatically pause playback while you sleep, fully supported by a battery-saving, true-black AMOLED dark mode.

How to Install the App (Step-by-Step)

If you accidentally deleted the app from your Xiaomi device, or if you want to try it on a Samsung or Pixel phone, follow these steps to get it running safely.

1

Check Google Play Store

Search for "Mi Music" in the official Google Play Store. If your device is compatible, install it directly from there for the safest and most updated version.

2

Download the APK (If Unavailable)

If the app says "Not compatible with your device", you will need to download the official APK file from a trusted repository like APKMirror.

3

Enable "Unknown Sources"

Go to your phone's Settings > Security > Install Unknown Apps. Grant permission to your web browser to install the downloaded APK file.

4

Install and Grant Permissions

Tap the downloaded file to install. Upon opening the app for the first time, grant it "Storage/Media" permissions so it can scan your phone for music files.
