Why Choose the Mi Music App?

Pre-installed on millions of MIUI and HyperOS devices globally, it provides a lightweight, battery-efficient local music listening experience.

🎧

Broad Format Decoding

Natively supports high-fidelity audio formats including FLAC and WAV without the need to download third-party audio codecs.

🎛️

Mi Sound Enhancer

Directly communicates with Xiaomi's hardware equalizer, offering specific acoustic presets designed to optimize output for your earbuds.

📜

Dynamic Lyrics

Automatically detects and displays local LRC files in your music directory so you can read along with the vocals while listening.

⏱️

Sleep Timer & Cutter

Built-in utilities allow you to set auto-shutoff timers for bedtime or easily trim your favorite chorus to use as a custom ringtone.
