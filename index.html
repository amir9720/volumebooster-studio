About GOODEV Audio Studio

GOODEV Audio Studio is a lightweight, browser-based digital audio workstation delivering instant sound utilities without software installation. All processing executes locally on client hardware using high-precision Web Audio API buffers, ensuring maximum user data privacy and zero upload latency.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is strictly respected. This web suite does NOT upload, store, or transmit your audio or video files to external servers. All operations—including gain amplification, frequency synthesis, filtering, and video extraction—are performed entirely inside your device's memory.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to our website. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Terms of Service

By using Volume Booster GOODEV Studio, you agree to utilize its sound-processing capabilities responsibly. High-gain amplification above 100% can generate substantial sound pressure; users are advised to monitor volume levels to protect physical hearing and prevent speaker driver damage.
