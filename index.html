Mi Music App: Features and Usage Guide

Welcome to the unofficial hub for Xiaomi's default audio player. Learn about its core capabilities, discover how to manage your local library effectively, and find solutions to common playback issues.

📱 Get it on Google Play

Why Choose the Mi Music App?

Pre-installed on millions of MIUI and HyperOS devices globally, the Mi Music application is engineered to provide a lightweight, battery-efficient local music listening experience. Unlike heavy subscription-based streaming platforms, it focuses heavily on playing your personal collection of MP3s, FLACs, and AAC files directly from your device's storage.

Verified Core Features

  • Broad Format Decoding: Natively supports high-fidelity audio formats including FLAC and WAV without the need to download third-party codecs.
  • Integrated Mi Sound Enhancer: Directly communicates with Xiaomi's hardware equalizer, offering specific acoustic presets designed to optimize output for various Xiaomi and Redmi earbuds.
  • Dynamic Lyrics Syncing: Automatically detects and displays LRC files in your music directory so you can read along with the vocals.
  • Sleep Timer & Ringtone Cutter: Built-in utilities allow you to set auto-shutoff timers for bedtime or trim your favorite chorus to use as a phone ringtone.
💡 Pro Tip: To get the absolute best audio quality from your Xiaomi device, go to your phone's Settings > Sound & Vibration > Sound Effects, and ensure 'Mi Sound Enhancer' is toggled on when using wired headphones.

Need to Edit Your Audio Files?

While the app is an excellent player, it does not include advanced audio editing features like volume boosting, 8D spatial panning, or vocal isolation.
