How Does Online Volume Booster Work?

Have you ever downloaded a webinar, voice note, or interview recording that was barely audible even at your phone's maximum loudness? Traditional media players simply output whatever amplitude was originally encoded into the audio file. Our online volume booster utilizes modern client-side digital signal processing (DSP) to amplify audio waveforms up to four times their original volume.
