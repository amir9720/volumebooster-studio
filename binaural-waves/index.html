How to Use This Tool

1

Wear Headphones

Binaural beats only work when your left and right ears are isolated. Using phone speakers will completely ruin the effect.

2

Select Your Goal

Use the top slider to choose a brainwave state. Select 'Delta' for sleep, 'Alpha' for relaxation, or 'Beta' for intense studying.

3

Adjust the Pitch

Use the bottom slider to choose a carrier tone that is pleasing to your ears. A lower tone (like 150 Hz) usually feels more relaxing.

4

Keep Volume Low

Binaural beats should be listened to at a low, comfortable volume. They are meant to be background ambiance, not loud music.

Frequently Asked Questions

When you play two slightly different frequencies in each ear (e.g., 200 Hz in the left ear and 210 Hz in the right ear), your brain detects the phase difference and creates a third "phantom" beat at exactly the difference between the two (10 Hz). This encourages your brainwaves to align with that frequency.
- Delta (1-4 Hz): Deep, dreamless sleep and physical healing.
- Theta (4-8 Hz): Deep meditation, REM sleep, and creativity.
- Alpha (8-13 Hz): Relaxation, light meditation, and reduced anxiety.
- Beta (13-30 Hz): Active concentration, studying, and problem-solving.
- Gamma (30+ Hz): Peak mental awareness and high-level information processing.
Binaural beats are generally safe for most people. However, individuals who suffer from epilepsy or seizures should consult a doctor before using brainwave entrainment tools, as rhythmic auditory stimulation can occasionally be a trigger.
