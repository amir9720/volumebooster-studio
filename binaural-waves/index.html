What Are Binaural Beats?

Binaural beats are an auditory illusion created when two slightly different pure tone frequencies are played separately into each ear through headphones. For example, if a 200 Hz tone is played in your left ear and a 204 Hz tone is played in your right ear, your brain processes the difference—a pulsing 4 Hz beat. This phenomenon encourages brainwave entrainment, gently guiding your mental state toward relaxation, focus, or deep sleep.

The MiMusic Binaural Beats Generator utilizes precise Web Audio API oscillators to generate these frequencies in real-time right inside your browser, completely free and without requiring external downloads or app installations.

Brainwave Frequency Categories Explained

Different frequency offsets target distinct states of consciousness:

  • Delta Waves (1 - 4 Hz): Associated with deep, dreamless sleep and physical body restoration.
  • Theta Waves (4 - 8 Hz): Linked to deep meditation, intuition, relaxation, and memory consolidation.
  • Alpha Waves (8 - 14 Hz): Promotes a calm, relaxed yet alert state ideal for light studying and creative work.
  • Beta Waves (14 - 30 Hz): Associated with active concentration, sharp alertness, and problem-solving.
