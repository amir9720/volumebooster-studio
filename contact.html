Common Questions Before You Write

Can you recover an audio file I edited on MiMusic.net?

No. Because all audio tools run 100% locally inside your browser's memory, your files are never uploaded to our servers. Once you close or refresh the tab, the processed audio is cleared from your browser.

Are you the official Xiaomi support desk?

No. MiMusic.net is an independent educational resource and web audio toolkit. We are not affiliated with Xiaomi Inc. For official hardware warranty claims, please visit Xiaomi's official support portal.

A tool isn't producing sound on my phone—what should I check?

Make sure your device is not in Silent/Mute mode (especially on iOS), disconnect background Bluetooth devices if testing phone speakers, and check our Troubleshooting Guide.
