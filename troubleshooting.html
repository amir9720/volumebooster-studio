App Troubleshooting Guide

Having trouble with your music player? We have compiled the most common issues users face with the app and provided quick, proven solutions.

Before you begin: Many app glitches can be solved simply by restarting your smartphone. Please try a quick reboot before attempting the more advanced fixes below.
🎵

Local Music Files Not Showing

You have downloaded MP3 files to your phone's internal storage, but the app's library remains empty or is missing specific tracks.

✅ How to Fix It:

  1. Open the app and go to Settings (usually a gear icon).
  2. Navigate to Advanced Settings.
  3. Look for Filter audio files.
  4. Ensure that the settings for "By size" and "By length" are turned off or set very low. Sometimes the app hides short or small audio files thinking they are ringtones or voice notes.
  5. Go back to the main screen, pull down to refresh, and let the media scanner run.
🛑

App Keeps Crashing or Freezing

The application closes unexpectedly while playing a song, or the interface completely freezes and becomes unresponsive to touch.

✅ How to Fix It:

  1. Go to your phone's main Settings > Apps > Manage Apps.
  2. Search for "Music" or "Mi Music" and tap on it.
  3. Tap on Clear Data at the bottom.
  4. Select Clear Cache (Do not select Clear All Data, as it might reset your custom playlists).
  5. Force stop the app and reopen it.
🔋

Music Stops Playing in Background

When you lock your screen or switch to another application, the music abruptly stops playing after a few minutes.

✅ How to Fix It:

  1. Go to your phone's main Settings > Battery.
  2. Find the battery optimization or background activity settings for individual apps.
  3. Locate the Music app in the list.
  4. Change its background setting to No Restrictions. MIUI's aggressive battery saver often kills background audio to save power.

General Support FAQ

The player requires embedded lyrics within the ID3 tags of the audio file, or a separate .lrc file located in the exact same folder with the exact same name as the audio file. If neither is present, no lyrics will be displayed.
On Xiaomi devices, you can often disable ads by opening the app Settings, going to Advanced Settings, and toggling off "Show ads" or "Receive recommendations."
If a specific track refuses to play but others work fine, the file might be corrupted. Try uploading that specific file to our free Audio Tools (like the Volume Booster) to see if the browser engine can decode it. If it fails there too, the file is broken.
