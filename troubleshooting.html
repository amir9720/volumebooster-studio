Troubleshooting Mi Music

Encountering glitches while listening to music can be frustrating. Here is our official guide to solving the most common Mi Music app errors.

Issue 1: Downloaded Songs Are Missing

If you downloaded an MP3 but it isn't showing up in your library, the media scanner likely missed it.

The Fix: Go to the app Settings > Advanced Settings > "Filter audio files". Ensure the "By size" and "By length" filters aren't hiding your track. Then, manually tap "Scan local music".

Issue 2: The App Crashes Randomly

Frequent crashes are usually caused by corrupted cache files after a MIUI system update.

The Fix: Navigate to your phone's Settings > Apps > Manage Apps > Music. Tap "Clear Data" and select "Clear Cache". (Do not worry, this will not delete your actual MP3 files).

Issue 3: Audio Plays but is Too Quiet

If the volume is maxed out but the song is still quiet, the original file was likely exported with low gain.

The Fix: The app cannot fix the file itself. Use our free online Volume Booster tool to safely amplify the file's waveform, then re-open the boosted file in Mi Music.
